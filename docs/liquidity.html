How to read this
  • Points are percentage points above what distance alone predicts. Under pure randomness, a cluster twice as close is hit first about twice as often, so "price taps clusters" is true of any level. The map only helps if it beats that.
  • With the trend: price above a rising 50-bar average (or below a falling one) and the heavy cluster lies in that direction. Part of this is plain momentum.
  • Sweep & reclaim: a candle wicks through a large amount of resting liquidity and closes back. Result is the move in the reversal direction afterwards, before fees (~0.1% round trip).
  • A 90% range that includes 0 means the result could be luck.

Method

  • Candles: Hyperliquid perpetual futures (perp volume tracks leveraged trading).
  • High-volume candle (over 1.5× its 50-bar average) → new leveraged positions assumed at its close. Liquidation prices added at 10×, 25×, 50×, 100× for longs (below) and shorts (above), weighted by volume.
  • Swing highs/lows (5 bars each side) → stop-loss liquidity just beyond them.
  • A level disappears once price trades through it; its weight halves every 250 bars.
  • This is an estimate, not exchange liquidation data. Treat clusters as likely targets, not certainties.
