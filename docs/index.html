How it works, and its limits
  • Runs every day on GitHub Actions for BTC, ETH and SOL: fetches daily prices (Bitstamp for BTC, Coinbase for ETH and SOL), rebuilds weekly features, re-runs the full walk-forward backtest (retraining every model), forecasts the next 23 weeks and logs the forecast.
  • Models: last-6-years return history, gradient-boosted quantile regression on cycle/momentum signals, the 40 most similar past weeks, and a long-run power-law trend (Bitcoin's growth slowing each cycle). The first three drive the next few weeks; the trend model takes over from about 6 months out because it was far more accurate at long horizons in the backtest. The 80% range is sized by Bitcoin's volatility over the last 26 weeks, scaled so it caught 80% of outcomes in the backtest in both the wild 2017–2022 years and the calmer 2023–now years. Beyond 1 year it uses the wider of that and a fixed calibration.
  • Long range warning: 3–4 year forecasts rest on very few independent cycles. In the 4-year backtest only about 38% of outcomes ended above the median, so treat the far end as optimistic.
  • 20,000 simulated paths (reshuffled 4-week blocks of real BTC moves) give the fan, the odds and the lowest-close estimates.
  • Limits: no news, ETF flows, Fed or liquidation data; weekly closes only (intraday wicks go lower); about three full cycles of history. Not financial advice.
